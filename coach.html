About the coach

Coaching since 1996, a level 2 national coach and registered chair umpire, Coach Pilate has represented KenRho Park Tennis Club at various leagues and tournaments, and has been involved in development programmes including Get Ahead and Move It.

Lessons and programmes

Private lessons

One-on-one coaching for children and adults, tailored to your level and goals.

Group lessons

Small-group sessions for friends, families and club members of similar ability.

Junior development squad

Coach Pilate runs the junior development squad at KenRho. Speak to the coach about places and times.
