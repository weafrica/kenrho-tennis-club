KenRho Park Tennis Club is a friendly community club at Rhodes Park in Kensington, Johannesburg. Courts are open seven days a week from 6am to 6pm, with social tennis every Saturday and Sunday from 7:30am and club teams competing in men's and women's leagues.

Welcome to KenRho Park.

We're a tennis club in Kensington, Johannesburg, welcoming any level of player — from total beginners to competitive athletes. Our six courts are open seven days a week from 6am to 6pm, with parking available on site.

Social tennis

Every Saturday and Sunday from 7:30am — an open, friendly session for members of all standards.

League tennis

Coaching

Resident coach Pilate Ngwenya, head coach at Sweatennis Academy, is available to take on players at every level.

Private & group lessons

Coach Pilate offers private and group lessons for children and adults, and runs the junior development squad at KenRho.

Coaching since 1996, a level 2 national coach and registered chair umpire, Coach Pilate has represented KenRho Park Tennis Club at various leagues and tournaments, and has been involved in development programmes including Get Ahead and Move It.

Book a lesson: 076 433 0722 (WhatsApp) · sweatennisacademy@gmail.com
